<h1 style="font-size: 30px; margin-top: 30px; margin-bottom: 24px">Winter Workshop 2026</h1>

The MIDL Young Researcher Board is proud to present the next online event within its annual cycle: The MIDL Winter Workshop 2026! Two workshops, specifically designed for young researchers, and exactly at the time of the (academic) year where you need them the most!

As in previous editions, the core idea is to prepare early career researchers for their contributions to MIDL 2027, be it paper submissions or reviewing. Thus, we will host two workshops on exactly these topics, including practical sessions to sharpen your research skills.

The Winter Workshop 2026 will take place on **23 November 2026** and **14 December 2026** as a virtual Zoom event. Like last year, it is divided into two workshop tracks. The event will be free of charge. However, a mandatory registration is necessary, and Zoom links will be sent out after registration.

---

## Workshops

The core idea of the Winter Workshop is to support young researchers in taking their first steps towards authoring a publication or serving as a reviewer. Therefore, the event hosts two workshop tracks that will be run on two different dates. Both workshops will feature an introductory tutorial followed by an open discussion forum. Due to the strategic date of the Winter Workshop, the participants will be directly prepared to submit to or review for MIDL 2027.

### Writing Workshop

We are happy to announce that the workshop will be led by Asst. Prof. Christian Baumgartner from University of Lucerne. In this session you will learn how to write a paper in the age of LLMs.

### Reviewing Workshop

We are excited to be joined by Dr. Emma Stanley from Imperial College London. She will talk about how and why to write a review (without Claude).
