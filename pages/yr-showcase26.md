<h1 style="font-size: 30px; margin-top: 30px; margin-bottom: 24px">MIDL Young Researcher Showcase 2026</h1>

The MIDL Young Researcher Board is delighted to welcome you to the 2026 MIDL Young Researcher Showcase!

The Young Researcher Showcase provides a platform for emerging researchers to share exciting work in progress, preliminary findings, and recent research with the MIDL community. This year's program brings together young researchers working across medical imaging, machine learning, image registration, and accessible AI.

Each presenter will have 6 minutes to present their work, followed by 2 minutes for questions and discussion.

We encourage attendees to engage with our presenters, ask questions, and help us celebrate the exciting research being conducted by the next generation of researchers in our community.

The MIDL Young Researcher Showcase will take place on **23 September, 14:00-15:00 UTC+0** as a virtual Zoom event. Please register using the link below to receive Zoom links for the event. The event will be free of charge.

{{ button("Registration Link", "https://forms.gle/R6UWhUgkkdUAnohA8") }}

All participants are invited to stay on the Zoom link after the Showcase for a social gathering if you so wish!
